# Video Note: Course Introduction

**Video length:** 1 min

## Overview

Welcome video for the course, introducing who the course is for, its prerequisites, and what each module covers.

## Who This Course Is For

- Aspiring AI developers, machine learning engineers, data scientists, AI researchers, or someone in a related role.
- **Prerequisites:** Python programming skills and experience are essential — the course dives straight into coding and application development.
- Fundamental AI and web development knowledge are highly recommended.

## Course Structure

1. **Module 1** — In-depth exploration of generative AI and prompt engineering, with a focus on the LangChain framework and its role in designing GenAI applications. Includes a hands-on lab building prompt templates using LangChain.
2. **Module 2** — Deeper dive into LangChain's core concepts and advanced features, with a hands-on lab demonstrating how LangChain simplifies integrating advanced AI capabilities into practical applications.
3. **Module 3** — A series of videos and readings to build a GenAI-powered web app using LangChain and Flask. Also covers evaluating different language models to determine which best suits your needs.

## How to Get the Most From This Course

- Watch the videos.
- Go through each reading.
- Check your understanding with practice quizzes.
- Perform hands-on labs.
- Test your knowledge with the graded assessment.

## Why This Matters

This video frames the course's trajectory: prompt engineering and LangChain fundamentals (Module 1) build toward integrating advanced LangChain capabilities (Module 2), which then culminate in a deployed, working GenAI web app (Module 3). The explicit call-out of Python and web development prerequisites signals this is an applied, hands-on-lab-heavy course rather than a purely conceptual one — worth keeping in mind when a later module assumes familiarity with Flask or LangChain's API surface without re-explaining it from scratch.
